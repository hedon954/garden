---
title: "Warm paper on a late-night monitor"
date: "2026-04-10T21:30:00+08:00"
---

Switched the editor and browser preview side by side at ten in the evening. Stepping the background down from `#ffffff` to `#faf9f5` in light mode — and `#1f1e1d` in dark mode — takes the glare right out of a wide display.
